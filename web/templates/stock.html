What this history records

These are saved stock adjustments, catalog edits, promotion and featured changes, and manager basket/order actions, plus the original order placement time. Recorded picking, status and shopper-assignment changes appear under Order changed. Automatic hold expiry and ordinary customer basket edits are not recorded as individual events. Historical stock before/after balances were not saved and are not reconstructed. Stock event product names use the current catalog name; catalog event names and order receipts retain their recorded details. Events recorded within the same minute have no exact cross-source ordering.
